Georgia, and what Android would use instead

Georgia ships on iOS and macOS. It does not exist on Android, so every heading, moment title and the brand mark silently falls back there. This page exists so the choice is made by looking rather than by argument. It does not make the choice. See issue #177.

Checking what your device actually has… If this message does not change, JavaScript is off and the Georgia rows below may be a fallback rather than Georgia.

Every other face on this page is embedded in this file, so it renders the same on any device. Georgia cannot be embedded — it is licensed, not open — so the Georgia rows are the one thing here that depends on your device. That is the whole problem in miniature.

Two things worth noticing before you choose

The product asks for a weight Georgia does not have. src/styles.css sets font-weight: 500 in fifteen places. Georgia ships Regular and Bold only, so today the browser rounds it down to Regular and the design has quietly been getting 400 everywhere. Lora, Source Serif 4 and Noto Serif are variable fonts with a real 500 — pick one and every heading gets slightly heavier than it is today, unless the weight is changed to 400 at the same time. Compare the 46px rows above: part of that difference is the 500, not the typeface.

The tracking is tuned to Georgia. h1, h2 carries letter-spacing: -0.035em, which suits Georgia's wide proportions. On a narrower face it reads as too tight. It would need re-testing, not carrying over.

The options, and what each one costs

  1. Bundle a serif with the app. Identity holds everywhere. Costs app size and a licence to record. A bundled file is arguably not what “no webfont” ever meant — but that is the amendment to write down, not to assume.
  2. Accept the platform serif per OS. Free, and it is what happens today by default. The Noto Serif rows above are what half the installs would get. Nothing to implement, nothing to licence, and the brand is different on Android forever.
  3. Change the face everywhere, web included. Most consistent, most disruptive. Concretely: 11 visual snapshots under tests/ would need regenerating, and the -0.035em tracking re-tuned.

Licence and size, for option 1

FaceLicenceSourceNote
LoraSIL Open Font License 1.1Google FontsVariable. Contemporary, slightly calligraphic; more stroke contrast than Georgia.
Source Serif 4SIL Open Font License 1.1Google Fonts / AdobeVariable. The most neutral of the three; closest to Georgia in colour on a page.
Noto SerifSIL Open Font License 1.1Google FontsVariable. Already on Android, so bundling it would change iOS only.
Charis SILSIL Open Font License 1.1SILStatic weights, so no real 500. Descends from Charter; built for long reading at small sizes.

All four are OFL 1.1, which permits bundling in an app. The subsets embedded in this page cover only the specimen text above, so their size here says nothing about a shipped bundle. Measure the real file before recording a number.

What this page is not. It does not choose, amend CLAUDE.md, or implement anything. #177 asks for a decision and its reasoning to be recorded by a person; TL-M-03 applies whatever is decided. The comparison is the only part that could be prepared in advance.
